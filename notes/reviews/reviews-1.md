# Sampler box reviews — file 1 of 6

## 5/5 — Priya N.

The Assam Breakfast was the standout. Strong enough to wake me up without milk, and I have already ordered a full tin.

## 4/5 — Tom B.

The Jasmine Green is lovely and light. The tin design is clever, though the lid takes two hands to open.

## 5/5 — Grace L.

I bought this to find out what I like, and it worked. The Oolong was a tea I would never have picked on my own.

## 3/5 — Ravi M.

The teas are good, but the box took nine days to arrive. I had forgotten I ordered it.

## 4/5 — Elena G.

The Darjeeling First Flush was my favourite by a mile. I only wish there had been more than one sachet of it.

## 5/5 — Mateo S.

Bought it as a gift for my sister and she has not stopped talking about the Chai. The little tasting card made it feel considered.

## 2/5 — Hannah F.

The outer box arrived crushed and one sachet had split, so the Earl Grey was loose in the bottom. The tea itself was fine.

## 4/5 — Daniel O.

The Masala Chai is genuinely good. The brewing instructions do not say whether to use milk, so I guessed.

## 5/5 — Maya P.

The Peppermint is the freshest I have had from a box. It smells like the garden.

## 3/5 — Samir K.

Nice selection, slow dispatch. It sat as "packed" for four days before it moved.

## 4/5 — Clara J.

The Rooibos has become my evening cup. No caffeine, and it does not go bitter if I forget it.

## 5/5 — Jonah R.

Best sampler I have tried. Ten teas, all drinkable, and none of them felt like filler.

## 4/5 — Leila H.

The Sencha is bright and grassy. My only complaint is that there was so little of it that I could not try it twice.

## 3/5 — Theo M.

The brewing instructions are confusing. One side says 80°C and the other says "just off the boil", which is not the same thing.

## 5/5 — Nina P.

The White Peony was a surprise. I had never had a white tea and now I want a whole box of it.

## 4/5 — Owen P.

Good value for trying new teas. I found two I will buy again and skipped three I now know I do not like.

## 5/5 — Yuki T.

The Genmaicha is toasty and comforting. It tastes like the tea my grandmother made.

## 2/5 — Sofia R.

The parcel was left out in the rain and the box was wet through. Two sachets were ruined and the card was pulp.

## 4/5 — Amara D.

The Earl Grey is reliable and not too perfumed. Exactly what I hoped for.

## 5/5 — Ben C.

The Lapsang Souchong is a proper smoky tea. I expected it to be a novelty and it is now my Sunday morning habit.
