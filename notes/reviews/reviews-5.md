# Sampler box reviews — file 5 of 6

## 5/5 — Iris K.

The Hojicha is the one I keep coming back to. Roasted, low in caffeine, and it still tastes good if I leave the bag in too long.

## 4/5 — Peter A.

The Ceylon is clean and bright. The box took eleven days to arrive, which is long enough that I nearly emailed to ask.

## 3/5 — Nadia S.

Good tea, confusing brewing instructions. The card says "one sachet per cup" and the tin says "one sachet per pot", and I have a small pot.

## 5/5 — Callum W.

The Moroccan Mint is exactly the cup I wanted after dinner. Fresh mint, not toothpaste mint.

## 2/5 — Hana M.

The box was damp when it arrived and the tasting card had gone mouldy at one corner. The sachets were mostly fine but it was unpleasant to open.

## 4/5 — Peter L.

Lovely Gunpowder Green, and it does not go bitter the way my old green tea did. I would have liked two sachets of it rather than one.

## 5/5 — Rosa V.

The Chamomile is a proper sleepy cup. Whole flowers, not dust.

## 3/5 — Ahmed R.

The teas are fine but my parcel sat at "packed" for five days. By the time it moved I had stopped looking forward to it.

## 4/5 — Lucy H.

The Keemun has a nice cocoa edge. I only wish the box said what temperature to use, because I guessed and got it wrong the first time.

## 5/5 — Michael T.

Ten teas and nine of them were good, which is a better hit rate than any sampler I have bought. The Nilgiri was the surprise.

## 2/5 — Bethan G.

The outer packaging was split down one side and two sachets had fallen out into the delivery bag. Nothing was lost but it looked careless.

## 4/5 — Kwame O.

The Rooibos is my new evening habit. The brewing card repeats itself on both sides, which is a small waste.

## 5/5 — Anika P.

The Silver Needle is delicate and sweet. I would never have bought it on its own and now I want a tin.

## 3/5 — Rory D.

Slow shipping and a dented tin. The tea inside was good, so I am not angry, just not impressed with the packing.

## 5/5 — Sofia B.

The Rose Black is beautiful. It smells like a garden and tastes like tea, not perfume, which is a hard thing to get right.

## 4/5 — Emeka N.

The Yunnan Golden is malty and smooth. There was only one sachet and I wanted three.

## 2/5 — Fiona C.

My box arrived wet through. The card was unreadable and the sachets smelled of damp cardboard. I could not tell which tea was which.

## 4/5 — Jacob S.

The English Breakfast is a solid everyday cup. Nothing clever, just reliable.

## 5/5 — Mei L.

The Vanilla Rooibos is the one my children ask for. No caffeine, and it does not need sugar.

## 4/5 — Owen F.

Good selection and a nice tasting card. The instructions for the Oolong say "near boiling" and the tin says 90°C, which sent me looking it up.
