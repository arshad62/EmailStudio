# Sampler box reviews — file 3 of 6

## 5/5 — Amara D.

The Nilgiri in this box was a tea I had never heard of and now buy monthly. Beautifully floral.

## 4/5 — Jonah R.

The selection is thoughtful and the tasting card is a nice touch. One sachet was split and loose tea was in the box.

## 3/5 — Nina P.

The green teas are good but they all taste similar to me. I would have liked a wider spread of styles.

## 5/5 — Yuki T.

The Hojicha is roasted and low in caffeine, perfect for the evening. I did not expect to like it as much as the Genmaicha.

## 4/5 — Idris B.

The teas arrived well sealed and fresh. Delivery was a day later than the email said.

## 2/5 — Ruth A.

The box arrived wet after a rainy delivery and the bottom had gone soft. Two sachets were damp.

## 5/5 — Maeve D.

Every tea in this box was drinkable without milk, which is rare. The Oolong was the best of them.

## 4/5 — Tomas N.

The Darjeeling is excellent. I would have liked two sachets because one cup is not enough to judge a tea.

## 5/5 — Nora W.

This is the sampler I recommend to friends. It is honest about what it is and the tea is genuinely good.

## 3/5 — Jonas L.

The brewing card says "one sachet per cup" but the sachets are large. My first cup was far too strong.

## 4/5 — Clara J.

The Peppermint is very fresh and the Chamomile is gentle. The box was slightly crushed on one edge but the tea was fine.

## 5/5 — Hannah F.

The Ceylon with a splash of milk is my new breakfast. Clean and bright, no bitterness.

## 3/5 — Owen P.

Slow shipping. It took eight days and I had to ask where it was.

## 5/5 — Leila H.

The White Peony is delicate and sweet. It feels like a treat rather than an everyday tea.

## 4/5 — Mateo S.

A good range and a fair price for what you get. I would like the option to buy more of a single tea afterwards.

## 2/5 — Sofia R.

The instructions for the Oolong are unclear. It says "a short steep" without saying how short, and I overbrewed it twice.

## 5/5 — Daniel O.

The smoky Lapsang is not for everyone but it is exactly for me. Two cups and I was converted.

## 4/5 — Priya N.

The Assam is the strongest in the box and the one I finished first. Nice malty finish.

## 5/5 — Elena G.

The Genmaicha and Hojicha together made this box worth it. Roasted teas are underrated.

## 3/5 — Samir K.

Fine tea, but there was too little of the Jasmine Green. One sachet is a taste, not a sample.
