# Sampler box reviews — file 2 of 6

## 3/5 — Idris B.

A fair introduction, but half the teas were green and I would have liked more black tea in the mix.

## 5/5 — Maeve D.

The Ceylon is clean and bright. I drank it without sugar for the first time in years.

## 4/5 — Tomas N.

The Chamomile is soft and not dusty. The box arrived with one corner dented, but nothing inside was harmed.

## 2/5 — Ruth A.

The brewing instructions contradict each other. The card says three minutes, the sachet says five. My first cup was bitter.

## 5/5 — Jonas L.

The Oolong is the reason I joined the club. It tastes of something, not just "tea".

## 4/5 — Nora W.

Lovely variety and a nice size for one person. Shipping was slower than I expected for a small parcel.

## 3/5 — Clara J.

I liked the Darjeeling but there was only one sachet. For a sampler, one is not enough to decide.

## 5/5 — Ravi M.

The Assam Breakfast is now the only tea in my kitchen. Strong, malty, and it takes milk well.

## 4/5 — Hannah F.

The peppermint and chamomile were the highlights. The box was a little soggy on one side when it arrived, though the sachets were sealed.

## 2/5 — Owen P.

The instructions on the green tea are wrong for the tea. I followed them and got a bitter cup, then looked it up online.

## 5/5 — Leila H.

The Jasmine Green is beautiful. I could smell it before I opened the sachet.

## 4/5 — Mateo S.

Good gift, quick enough delivery. My mother has already asked which one is the "fancy" one.

## 3/5 — Sofia R.

The tea is fine but the box is hard to reseal. Once opened, the sachets slide out every time.

## 5/5 — Daniel O.

The Masala Chai with milk and honey is the best thing in my cupboard. I have made it four evenings running.

## 4/5 — Priya N.

A good way to work out what I like. I would have liked a second sachet of the White Peony.

## 1/5 — Theo M.

The parcel arrived wet, the box had collapsed, and the tasting card was unreadable. The tea was replaced quickly, but the first impression was poor.

## 5/5 — Elena G.

The Genmaicha is a lovely everyday tea. Toasty, cheap to drink, and it does not need anything added.

## 4/5 — Samir K.

The Rooibos is a good evening option. The tin was dented in transit but the seal held.

## 5/5 — Grace L.

I have tried a lot of samplers and this is the first where I liked every single tea. The Lapsang was the surprise.

## 3/5 — Ben C.

Nice teas, but the delivery took over a week and there was no update in between.
