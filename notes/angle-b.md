# Angle B — discovering new teas

Three subject line options for email 01 ("The club is open"). The angle is the
new tea itself: the small surprise of trying something you have not had before.
Not the ritual (that is Angle A), and not a deadline.

| # | Subject line | Characters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A new tea to discover each month | 32 |
| 2 | Meet a tea you have not tried yet | 33 |
| 3 | New teas to try, chosen for you | 31 |

All three are under the 50-character limit in `.opencode/rules/emails.md`.

## Why these words

- "New", "try" and "discover" are on the brand's words-we-use list in
  `brand/voice.md`. "Chosen for you" is the plan's own positioning line
  (section 3), so it is safe to echo.
- No count of teas is promised. The plan says box contents are not confirmed
  (section 9), so none of these lines say how many teas arrive.
- No price, discount, testimonial, customer count or deadline. No health claim,
  no ranking claim and nothing from the avoid list.
- Each one invites a taste rather than announcing a sale.

## Why they differ from Angle A

Angle A sells the quiet moment ("a quiet cup", "one small ritual"). Angle B
sells the new tea. They are separate bets on what makes a waitlist member open
the email, and they should not be mixed in one line.
