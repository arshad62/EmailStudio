# Handoff — email 01 subject line

- Tea-curious beginners: permission to be new, a simple first step, no test.
- Gift buyers: the facts before they commit — how a gift works, when it arrives, what it costs.
- Daily drinkers: something new inside the cup they already make.
- Angle A (calm daily ritual): "A quiet cup, every day" / "One small ritual, once a month" / "Slow down with a monthly cup".
- Angle B (discovering new teas): "A new tea to discover each month" / "Meet a tea you have not tried yet" / "New teas to try, chosen for you".
- Pick Angle B, "A new tea to discover each month".
- Why: the subject is shared across all three groups, and "a new tea" is the one idea that fits each — new for daily drinkers, a gentle start for beginners, something to pass on for gift buyers.
- Angle A's ritual is warmer for daily drinkers only, so it is the weaker shared bet.
