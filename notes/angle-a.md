# Angle A — calm daily ritual

Three subject line options for email 01 ("The club is open"). The angle is the
quiet moment in the day, not the tea itself and not a deadline.

| # | Subject line | Characters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A quiet cup, every day | 22 |
| 2 | One small ritual, once a month | 30 |
| 3 | Slow down with a monthly cup | 28 |

All three are under the 50-character limit in `.opencode/rules/emails.md`.

## Why these words

- "Quiet", "small" and "monthly cup" come from the brand's own words and phrases in
  `brand/voice.md` ("a quiet moment", "small", "cup"). "Ritual" is not on that
  list; it echoes the on-voice example "a small ritual you can keep".
- "Slow down" is close to the brand's "slow", which is on the list. It is the
  plainest way to say "pause" without sounding like a spa.
- None of them promise a health benefit, a price, a discount or a deadline. The
  plan rules all of those out.
- Each one reads as an invitation, not an instruction. No countdown, no "last
  chance", no "don't miss out".
