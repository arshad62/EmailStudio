# Kettlebird Tea — brand voice

## Our voice in three words

**Warm. Plain. Curious.**

We sound like a friend who knows tea, not a company trying to sell something. We are never loud, never smug, and never in a hurry.

## Words we use

- tea, leaves, cup, pot, steep, brew
- small, everyday, slow, calm, cosy
- try, taste, notice, compare, explore
- we, you, our, your
- honest, simple, fresh
- "a good cup", "a quiet moment", "something new"

## Words we avoid

- revolutionary, game-changing, disrupt, unlock, secret, hack
- best, number one, world-class, unbeatable
- cheap, deal, hurry, last chance, don't miss out
- detox, cleanse, toxins, superfood, miracle
- skinny, weight loss, fat-burning, boosts metabolism
- cure, treat, prevent, doctor-recommended, clinically proven
- guaranteed, risk-free, forever

## Claims we must never make

We must never say or imply that tea:

- detoxes, cleanses or removes toxins
- cures, treats or prevents any illness or condition
- helps you lose weight or burn fat
- is recommended by doctors or proven by clinical trials
- is the best tea in the world or guaranteed to please
- will change your life, your body or your health

We also must never invent a price, a discount, a testimonial, a customer count or a deadline. If we do not know a fact, we leave it out or ask.

## How we sign off

Use this exact sign-off at the end of every marketing email:

```text
Warm cups,
The Kettlebird Tea team
```

## Two examples

**Too loud:** "Unlock the secret to a detoxed body with our revolutionary tea — last chance!"
**On voice:** "A quiet cup, a new tea, and a small ritual you can keep. That is The Steep Club."

**Too pushy:** "Don't miss out — guaranteed to be the best tea you've ever tasted."
**On voice:** "If you have been curious about the club, this is a simple way to try the first box."
