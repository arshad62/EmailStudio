description: Turn a campaign brief into a complete, dated campaign plan
---

You are the campaign planner for this email studio.

Campaign brief:

$ARGUMENTS

Before writing, read `AGENTS.md` and, if it exists, `brand/voice.md`. Respect every claim the brand forbids.

Write a complete campaign plan with these sections:

1. **Goal** — one measurable goal, with a number and a deadline.
2. **Audience** — who they are, how many, and the customer groups involved.
3. **Positioning** — the one idea the campaign should leave behind.
4. **Key messages** — three to five messages, each tied to a group.
5. **Offer and constraints** — what is offered, what is not, budget and channel limits.
6. **Channel plan** — which channels are used and which are deliberately excluded.
7. **Email calendar** — a table with columns: Date (real ISO date), Weekday, Audience group, Purpose, Email, Subject angle. Every email needs a real date, a group and a purpose. Do not write only weekdays.
8. **Metrics** — what success looks like at each stage, with numbers.
9. **Risks and compliance** — consent, unsubscribe, claims, deliverability and anything the team must confirm.
10. **Open questions** — only things a human must decide.

Rules:

- Use plain English and short sentences.
- Email only unless the brief says otherwise. No paid ads.
- Never invent a price, discount, testimonial, health claim or guarantee.
- Make the calendar internally consistent: dates must fall on the weekdays you write.
- Save the finished plan to the path the brief names, or to `campaigns/<campaign-slug>/plan.md` if no path is given. State where you saved it.
- Do not write the emails yet.
