# Rules for this folder

These are made-up practice people. They exist so the studio can rehearse a launch without emailing anyone.

## Hard rules

1. Never add a real person or a real email address to `leads.csv`.
2. Every email address must end in `@example.com`. That domain is reserved and cannot receive mail.
3. Never change an existing `@example.com` address. If a test needs a different address, make up a new one.
4. Every row needs a `consent` value. Do not leave it blank, and do not invent a consent type that the studio did not collect.
5. Keep the exact columns: `first_name,last_name,email,group,joined_on,consent`.
6. Ask before deleting or rewriting rows.

## Groups

- `tea-curious` — people who like the idea of tea but do not know where to start. They need permission to be new.
- `gift-buyers` — people looking for a gift, often for someone else. They need clear gifting facts and timing.
- `daily-drinkers` — people who already drink tea every day. They need something new inside a habit they already have.

## If someone asks for a real address

Stop. Quote rules 1 and 2. Offer to add a made-up practice person at `@example.com` instead, and only after the studio owner agrees.
